• New: LUKS1 and LUKS2 encrypted volumes are now supported alongside VeraCrypt
• New: ext2, ext3 and ext4 filesystems are now supported (read-only)
• New: The unlock form automatically detects whether a partition is LUKS or VeraCrypt and shows only the relevant fields
• New: Drives with no recognisable encrypted partitions now display a clear "no encrypted volumes found" message instead of a confusing empty list
• New: Mounted drive cards show the device name and partition, with colour-coded tags for encryption type and filesystem
