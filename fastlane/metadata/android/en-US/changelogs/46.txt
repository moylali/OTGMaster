• New: LUKS1 and LUKS2 encrypted volumes are now supported alongside VeraCrypt
• New: ext2, ext3 and ext4 filesystems are now supported — ext4 is read/write, ext2 and ext3 are read-only, and FAT32 and exFAT are unchanged
• New: On ext4 you can create files and directories, write and modify data, delete, and rename
• New: The unlock form automatically detects whether a partition is LUKS or VeraCrypt and shows only the relevant fields
• New: Drives with no recognisable encrypted partitions now display a clear "no encrypted volumes found" message instead of a confusing empty list
• New: Mounted drive cards show the device name and partition, with colour-coded tags for encryption type and filesystem
• New: The volume picker tags each partition with its encryption type, so you can tell LUKS1, LUKS2 and VeraCrypt apart at a glance
• New: The volume picker lists only partitions you can actually unlock, and says so, instead of offering ones that could never be mounted
• Fixed: Unencrypted partitions were labelled "VERACRYPT" in the volume picker, and selecting one produced a misleading "failed to unlock" instead of saying it is not encrypted
• Fixed: On a drive holding both encrypted and unencrypted partitions, the encrypted partition could become unreachable after dismissing the Auto mount prompt
• Fixed: On drives with several encrypted partitions, some partitions could fail to mount, and unmounting one could disconnect the others
• Fixed: A temporary USB read error no longer discards the credentials you asked the app to remember for a partition
