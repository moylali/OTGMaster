OTG Master lets you mount VeraCrypt and LUKS-encrypted USB drives directly on your Android device — no root required.

Connect an encrypted volume via USB OTG, enter your password or use your fingerprint, and access your encrypted files through Android's built-in Files app. Unmount safely when done.

FEATURES
• Multiple Encryption Formats — mount VeraCrypt, LUKS1, and LUKS2 volumes
• Broad File System Support — read and write files on FAT32, exFAT, and ext4 partitions
• Read-Only Mode — optionally mount partitions safely without risking accidental modifications
• Auto Mount — automatically unlocks known drives on connect using biometric authentication
• Hub Support — multiple USB drives connected via a hub are all prompted in a single biometric step
• Keyfile Support — use a keyfile in addition to or instead of a password
• Custom PIM — supports non-default PIM values for stronger key derivation
• Cipher Selection — AES and Serpent supported
• Partitioned Disks — scans MBR/GPT partition tables and lets you pick the exact volume to unlock
• No Root Required — works on standard, unmodified Android devices
• No Cloud, No Tracking — your files never leave your device

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later
• USB OTG cable or adapter
• A VeraCrypt or LUKS-encrypted USB drive

OTG Master is free and open source.
